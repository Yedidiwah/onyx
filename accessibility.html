ONYX Radar

Accessibility Statement

ONYX Radar is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards.

Measures to support accessibility

  • Including accessibility throughout our internal policies.
  • Using semantic HTML5 elements for easier screen-reader navigation.
  • Ensuring high contrast ratios for text readability.

Feedback

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of ONYX Radar. If you encounter accessibility barriers, please let us know via our Telegram support channel.
